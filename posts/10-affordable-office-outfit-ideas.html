10 Affordable Office Outfit Ideas

You do not need a new outfit every day to look polished. Keep a small set of reliable formulas and change the details.

Blazer, tee and tailored trousers

A simple tee softens a structured blazer. Add straight or wide-leg trousers and loafers.

Button-down, trousers and flats

Keep the shirt crisp and let accessories add personality.

Knit top and midi skirt

A fine-gauge knit gives a softer alternative to a button-down.

Dress and blazer

One dress can become several outfits when you rotate the outer layer and shoes.

Monochrome neutrals

Similar tones create a clean line; use jewelry, shoes or a bag for contrast.

Make the formulas your own

Save combinations that fit your dress code and repeat them.
