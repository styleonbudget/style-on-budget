Plan work outfits before you shop

  1. Check your workplace dress code and weekly schedule.
  2. Start with tops, trousers, skirts or dresses you already wear.
  3. Try combinations and take photos of the ones that work.
  4. Make a short list of missing items and set a realistic spending limit.
