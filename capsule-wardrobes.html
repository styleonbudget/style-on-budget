Capsule wardrobes

Build a budget capsule wardrobe that works for your life

A capsule wardrobe is a flexible collection of clothes that work well together. You do not need to buy a matching set or follow a strict item count. Start with what you own, choose pieces that suit your routine, and add only what fills a real gap.

These capsule wardrobe guides focus on practical outfit planning, versatile basics and budget-conscious choices. Use them to make more combinations from fewer pieces without losing your personal style.

How to start a capsule wardrobe without overspending

  1. Shop your closet first. Set aside the clothes you wear and feel good in.
  2. Look for repeatable combinations. Choose tops and bottoms that work together in more than one way.
  3. Keep colours personal. Pick shades you enjoy and that coordinate with clothes you already own.
  4. Identify real gaps. Make a short list before shopping and set a spending limit.
  5. Plan outfits for your actual week. Consider weather, comfort, dress code and laundry.
